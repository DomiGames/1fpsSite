About the game

NEPA Wahala is a shop management game about keeping the lights on when the grid gives up. The power has gone, the street is dark, and Mama T Provisions is still open for business.

Serve the customers queuing up outside, and keep the generator fed so the shop stays lit. Make it through to closing time and you can upgrade your shop, then open again and push further than you did last night.

It runs right here in your browser. Nothing to install.

  • Run Mama T Provisions: serve the queue and keep the shop trading through the blackout
  • Mind the generator: keep it fuelled, because a dead gen means a dark shop
  • Reach closing time: survive the night, then upgrade your shop
  • Best-time chase: open again and push further than last night
  • Plays in your browser: free, no download, works on desktop and phone

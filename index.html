The studio

One tiny studio,
three very different worlds.

1FPS Games is a small indie game studio making games we'd actually want to play. No publishers, no hype, no filler.

So far that means a lone ship dodging geometric doom inside a dome, a chaotic bullet-hell about a burger taking back the restaurants, and a quiet, minimal climb to the top of the world. Three games, nothing alike. All polished until they shine.

About the game

Black Ballz is a go-up platformer where your health determines how many jumps you can make. Battle the bosses to earn rewards or ignore them: the choice is yours. Reach the top and discover the hidden mystery of this world.

  • Jump-Based Health: your health is your jumps
  • Boss Battles: fight or ignore them, your choice
  • Mystery to Unlock: reach the top to discover the truth
  • Minimalist Design: clean, atmospheric, challenging

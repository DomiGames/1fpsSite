About the Game

Black Ballz is a go‑up platformer game where your health determines how many jumps you can make. Battle bosses to get rewards or ignore them – the choice is yours. Reach the top and discover the hidden mystery of this world.

  • Jump-Based Health – Your health is your jumps
  • Boss Battles – Fight or ignore them, your choice
  • Mystery to Unlock – Reach the top to discover the truth
  • Minimalist Design – Clean, atmospheric, challenging

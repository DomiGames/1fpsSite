About the game

Dome 3.0 is a bullet hell roguelite survival shooter. You are a lone ship trapped inside a dome, and endless waves of geometric enemies close in from every direction. Dodge. Dash. Destroy. How long can you survive?

Level up mid-run and pick 1 of 3 abilities: piercing rounds, ricochet shots, orbital lasers, homing missiles, bullet time, and dozens more. Six ships (Apex, Nova, Wraith, Fortress, Swarm, and Prism) each bring their own stats and a unique Full Power ultimate. Every run builds a different ship.

Dodge at the last possible moment and the whole world slows to a crawl in black and white, while you keep moving at full speed. Clear the campaign to unlock endless survival mode, then compete on global leaderboards.

  • 20 Unique Bosses: from the Circle Architect to the final Dome Zero, no two fights play the same
  • 50 Roguelite Upgrades: pick 1 of 3 every level-up, with permanent upgrades between runs
  • 6 Ships, 6 Playstyles: each with its own stats and a unique Full Power ultimate
  • Bullet Time: dodge at the last moment and the world slows around you
  • Endless Survival Mode: waves never stop. Neither do you.
  • Compete: achievements and global leaderboards

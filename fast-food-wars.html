About the game

Fast Food Wars is a bullet-hell shoot'em up rogue-lite set in procedurally generated restaurants with random power-ups and weapons. You play as a burger fighting to claim back invaded burger restaurants from other fast foods.

Dash between enemy fire, slice them up to bits, shoot your enemies into oblivion, and survive chaotic encounters across procedurally generated levels that change every run. Collect powerful buffs, weapons, and special abilities to build crazy synergies that help you dominate the battlefield.

Clear all rooms in a restaurant and fight the boss of that restaurant. No run is the same with procedurally generated levels, weapons, and buffs.

  • No Ads: play without interruptions
  • No Gems or Premium Currency: fair, honest design
  • One-Time Purchase: play the level, then buy the full game once
  • Procedurally Generated: every run is unique
  • Crazy Synergies: combine power-ups for wild combos
